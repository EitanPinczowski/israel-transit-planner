- **Quick Settings tile "Home":** pull down the top panel to see your next trip home ("Home · 22:40–23:35"), read from saved results with no data used. Tap it to open the app planning home from where you are.
- **How to add it:** Settings → "Add "Home" to Quick Settings" (Android 13 and up). On older phones, pull the panel down twice, tap the pencil and drag "Home" into your tiles. Set a Home place first (🏠 next to a saved place).
