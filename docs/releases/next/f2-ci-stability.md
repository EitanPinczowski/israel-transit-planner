- **No crash when the phone's storage is full.** On a full phone the app could close as it opened, or when you saved a place or a setting. Now it opens and searches as usual; a change that doesn't fit is not saved and the app says so ("Not saved: phone storage is full"), everything saved before stays, and the background checks (night refresh, last trip) wait until there is room.
- **The map stays visible at the largest text size.** With very large text, the credit line above the results could wrap onto several lines and push the results over most of the map; the results now make room for it.
