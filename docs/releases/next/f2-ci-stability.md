- **No crash when the phone's storage is full.** Saving a place or a setting on a full phone used to close the app; now that one change is simply not saved, and everything saved before stays.
- **The map stays visible at the largest text size.** With very large text, the credit line above the results could wrap onto several lines and push the results over most of the map; the results now make room for it.
