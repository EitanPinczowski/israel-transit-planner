- **Your search survives.** If Android closes the app in the background, it comes back with the same From, To, time, tab, chosen option and open trip sheet, read from the saved results instead of searching again.
- **Map and list work together.** The chosen option is drawn on top in full colour, the others thin and faded; tap a faded route to pick its card, and the list scrolls to it.
- **Actions within thumb reach.** Start trip, Remind me and Send to driver now sit in one row at the bottom of the results, with a short vibration on Start trip, on dropping a pin and when the chosen option warns of a tight change (only if touch feedback is on).
