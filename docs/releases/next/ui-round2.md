- Typing a place happens right in the row you tapped, with the suggestions just under it and above the keyboard; one Back closes the search.
- All five tabs (Trip, Better start, Drop-off, Pick-up, Park & ride) now show at once, even on a small phone: no more scrolling sideways to find one.
- In landscape and on short phones, opening the search no longer squeezes it under the results: the results come back after the next search or with Back.
- Known issue: on Android 8, Back may not close an open search; tap the ✕ in the field, or pick a place.

- בעיה ידועה: באנדרואיד 8, "חזרה" עלולה לא לסגור חיפוש פתוח; הקישו על ✕ שבשדה, או בחרו מקום.
