- **Will my reminder ring?** Settings → Check reminders lists anything on your phone that could stop a reminder (notifications, alarms, battery, background limits, location), worst first, each with a button to the exact settings page. "Test reminder in 1 minute" goes through the real alarm and shows when it rang, or what most likely stopped it.
- **Rav-Kav pass ends on.** Type your monthly pass's last day in Settings: the app tells you 3 days before and on the last day, at 09:00. No internet needed.
