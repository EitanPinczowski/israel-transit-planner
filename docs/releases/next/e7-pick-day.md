- Plan a trip for another day: after "Depart" or "Arrive by", pick Today, Tomorrow, a weekday or any date up to 60 days ahead (Shabbat, Fridays and holidays are marked), then the time.
- The time chip now shows the day ("Depart tomorrow 08:15"), and a time that has already passed today asks "Tomorrow at …?" instead of guessing.
