**Trip helpers, alerts, Quick Settings tile, language & theme · עוזרי נסיעה, התראות, אריח הגדרות מהירות, שפה ומראה**

- **One-tap update:** when a new version is out, the banner says "Version 0.8.0 is ready · Update". One tap downloads it inside the app and opens Android's own "Update this app?" prompt. No browser, no hunting for the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow this app to install updates.
- **Check for updates:** Settings shows your version and a "Check for updates" button that asks right away.
- **Safe download:** the app installs only the exact file GitHub published (its checksum must match). If something goes wrong, the banner says why, with "Try again" and the browser as a fallback.
- **Mode tabs on one line:** Trip, Better start, Drop-off, Pick-up and Park & ride are now one row of icons, even on small phones and with large text. The chosen mode is highlighted with its name under it. Long-press an icon to see its name.
- Place search shows the town under every suggestion ("הרצל 10 · חיפה"), so the same street in two towns can be told apart, and typing the town after the street ("הרצל חיפה") puts that town's answers first.
- **Earlier / Later:** under the trip options, "Earlier" and "Later" load the next set of options and add them to the list (the sort chips apply to all of them).
- **If I miss it:** the selected option says when the next one leaves and how much later it arrives ("If I miss this: next at 08:35 (+30 min)").
- **Platform and stop number:** where you board and get off, buses show "Platform 12 · floor 6 · stop 47899" when the timetable has it (trains have no platform data).
- **Your usual trip:** a trip you took at least 3 times lately says how long it usually takes ("Usually 52 min · this one +8"), and a place's routine shows when it is quickest to leave ("Quickest when you leave around 07:15").
- **Monthly pass advice:** History says whether a monthly pass would have saved money last month, or paying per ride was cheaper. It is an estimate from the trips you started in the app only.
- **Quick Settings tile "Home":** pull down the top panel to see your next trip home ("Home · 22:40–23:35"), read from saved results with no data used. Tap it to open the app planning home from where you are.
- **How to add it:** Settings → "Add "Home" to Quick Settings" (Android 13 and up). On older phones, pull the panel down twice, tap the pencil and drag "Home" into your tiles. Set a Home place first (🏠 next to a saved place).
- **Leave-now countdown:** when a "time to leave" reminder goes off, its notification stays and counts down to boarding ("Line 5 · board at Rager/Oren · walk 6 min"), with "Start trip" and "Dismiss". It goes away 2 minutes after boarding, when the ride starts, or when you cancel the reminder.
- **Last trip home (opt-in):** Settings → "Last trip home alert". On an evening away from Home, a notification 30 minutes before you need to leave for the last trip home ("Last trip home 23:10 from Be'er Sheva Central: leave in 30 min"), with when service resumes before Shabbat or a holiday. Tap it to plan the trip home. It never uses your location in the background.
- **Language and theme:** Settings → Appearance picks the app's language (System / עברית / English) and theme (System / Light / Dark), applied at once. Stop names and searches follow the app's language. On Android 8–12, notifications stay in the phone's language.
- **Welcome tour:** the first launch shows a short tour of each mode, saved places and reminders. Skip it any time, or see it again from Settings.
- **Leg colours:** two buses in one trip now get two colours, the same on the map, its stops, the list and the trip's stop sheet.
- **Installing 0.8.0:** install this version once the usual way (download the APK below). From 0.8.0 on, updates are one tap inside the app.
- Known issue: on Android 8, Back may not close an open search; tap the ✕ in the field, or pick a place.

- **מוקדם / מאוחר:** מתחת לאפשרויות הנסיעה, "מוקדם יותר" ו"מאוחר יותר" טוענים עוד אפשרויות ומוסיפים אותן לרשימה (המיון חל על כולן).

- **כדאיות חופשי חודשי:** בהיסטוריה כתוב אם חופשי חודשי היה חוסך כסף בחודש שעבר, או שתשלום לפי נסיעה היה זול יותר. זו הערכה לפי הנסיעות שהתחלת באפליקציה בלבד.

- **איך מוסיפים:** הגדרות ← "הוספת "הביתה" להגדרות המהירות" (אנדרואיד 13 ומעלה). בטלפונים ישנים יותר גוררים את הלוח למטה פעמיים, מקישים על העיפרון וגוררים את "הביתה" לאריחים. קודם מגדירים מקום כבית (🏠 ליד מקום שמור).
