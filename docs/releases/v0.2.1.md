**Smaller, faster, and fixes from real routing data · קטנה ומהירה יותר, ותיקונים מנתוני אמת**

- The download is about half the size: the app now ships only what phones use.
- Stops on the map are kept on the phone for a week, so they appear instantly after reopening the app.
- **Drop me off:** works on long drives (it used to lose the car route past 30 minutes), and now offers train stations all along the way instead of only near the ends.
- **Better start:** better drop-off points, and the time to leave now allows for traffic.
- **Search:** Israeli streets come first (searching "רגר" no longer finds Zagreb).
- Trips on another day (e.g. over Shabbat) show the day, and absurdly long waits are hidden.
- Train trips show the destination instead of the train number.
