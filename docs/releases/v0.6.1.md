**Fixes · תיקונים**

- After the automatic refresh, the trip you picked stays picked, even when buses that already left drop off the list or "walk only" was the choice. Before, the selection could move to another bus, so "Start trip" or "Remind me" could follow the wrong one.
- A reminder on one part of an errand trip is re-checked for that part, so it no longer warns that the bus changed and then disappears. A reminder you cancel while it is being re-checked stays cancelled.
- With stops on the way, "Arrive by" is hidden (the trip is planned forward from when you leave), and "By car?" and "last trip" no longer answer for the direct trip instead.
- Opening a saved place from the app icon no longer comes back after rotating the phone or switching dark mode.
- The spoken get-off alert stays quiet when the phone has no voice for its language, instead of reading Hebrew with an English voice. The vibration and notification still come.
- Hebrew: arrows between places point the right way.
