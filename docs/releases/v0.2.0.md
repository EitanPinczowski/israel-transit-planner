**New: fare estimate · חדש: הערכת מחיר נסיעה**

- Every option now shows what the ride should cost, e.g. **≈ ₪8**: normal trips, better start, drop me off and pick me up.
- Settings → **Fare**: Regular, 50% discount, or Free (67+).
- Based on the Rav-Kav price list in force since April 2025: distance bands, free transfers within 90 minutes for rides up to 15 km, train prices, and the daily cap. Tap the note under the results for the official prices.
- Test builds no longer block installing the real app.
